Rawon

Back to the collection ↗

Ingredients

  • 1 lb beef brisket
  • 2 cups coconut milk
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, minced
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. Place the beef brisket in a pot and cover with water. Bring to a boil and skimming any foam that rises to the surface.
  2. Add the coconut milk, onion, garlic, ginger, bay leaves, salt, and black pepper.
  3. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 2-3 hours or until the beef is tender.
  4. Remove the bay leaves and adjust seasoning if needed.
