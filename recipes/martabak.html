Martabak

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted

Instructions:

  1. Mix flour, sugar, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. In another bowl, whisk together warm water, eggs, vanilla, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix until a smooth dough forms.
  4. Let the dough rest for 30 minutes.
  5. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  6. Roll out the dough on a floured surface to about 1/4 inch thickness.
  7. Cut into circles using a glass or cookie cutter.
  8. Place on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  9. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until golden brown.
