Nasi Goreng

Back to the collection ↗

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 2 tbsp kecap manis
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  2. Add garlic and shallot, sauté until fragrant.
  3. Pour in beaten eggs and scramble until cooked through.
  4. Add cooked rice and stir to combine.
  5. Add turmeric powder, kecap manis, salt, and pepper. Stir well.
  6. Cook for another 5 minutes or until heated through.
